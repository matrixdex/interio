How to use

Generate a kitchen

  1. Fill in any of the fields on the left, or none.
  2. Press Generate AI kitchen design. Three designs appear on the right.
  3. Click one to open it in 3D.

Tap a selected option again to clear it. Clear resets the whole brief; Empty room starts with bare walls instead. The dot on Claude at the top shows whether Claude is connected. Without it, designs come from the collection and are tagged Collection.

Type to it

The bar at the bottom of the 3D view takes precise commands. Press / to focus it and ↑ to bring back the last command.

  • move the window 50 cm left · lower the window 10 cm · move the door 20 cm right
  • move wall to the right · increase room size by 50 cm · room 12x10 ft · raise the ceiling 20 cm
  • add a tall pantry on the east wall · make the hob 90 with 5 burners · move the fridge left
  • countertop marble · tiles moroccan dado · top view · what is the price
  • undo · redo

Left and right mean as you face that wall from inside the room. Common phrasings run instantly without AI; anything else goes to Claude when it is connected. Requests outside the kitchen get “Doesn’t look like anything to me.”

Keep and share

Designs save automatically in this browser and appear under Saved at the bottom of the home screen. Summary shows the part list with prices.
