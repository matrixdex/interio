01

The System

A kitchen is a sentence in a language of modules. Twelve parts, a 10 cm grid, and rules the studio never breaks: a chimney over every hob, nothing across a door, the sink and the hob never left out.

01.1

Nine lines make a kitchen

Claude writes the intent: walls, the order of modules, the materials. The studio does the geometry, to the centimetre. Less to write, less to go wrong — a design in seconds.

02

Compose · Configure · Fabricate

  1. i.

    Compose

    Describe the room, or type to it — “move the window 50 cm left”, “room 12 × 10 ft”. Pinch, orbit, drag modules in like pieces on a board.

  2. ii.

    Configure

    Every module is parametric: width, fronts, burners, bowls, finishes. The price follows each choice, live.

  3. iii.

    Fabricate

    The finished drawing is a part list — module codes, sizes, finishes — ready for the workshop.

03
